**"Rare" or "Precious" raw materials** — like gold, lithium, cobalt, etc. These are often called:

- **Critical raw materials** – materials essential to production that have a risk of supply shortage
- **Scarce inputs** – limited natural resources used in manufacturing
- **Strategic materials** – materials so important and limited that their shortage can disrupt entire production lines
