# Interactive demos

## Gifs
People say that a photo is a 1000 words. However a [gif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIF) is million words. Gifs are perfect way to present a short and sweet feature. Demos need quickly and efficiently to show to the viewer what this feature or product is all about. And in other words filled witch short form content people don't have the attention span to watch a in depth tutorial of your product and how it work. Its better to split the feature or products into small gifs with a detailed description attached. This method attracts the viewer with a flashy 3second  demo and then presents them with a in depth description to solidify how the feature works in the viewers mind.
